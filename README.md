# TrackIt

A personal financial tracking app that helps you stay on top of your money with ease.

## Features

- 🏦 **Bank Integration** - Connect your accounts securely with Plaid
- 📈 **Smart Analytics** - Visualize your financial health with charts  
- 👥 **Household Sharing** - Collaborate with family members

## About

TrackIt is an iOS app built with SwiftUI that makes personal finance management simple and collaborative.
